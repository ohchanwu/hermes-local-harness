# Authority and review

The shared tracked skill is canonical; the `~/.hermes` copies are deployed state. Apply only through supported Hermes commands or a manual copy after explicit authorization. Each implementation change requires the board-designated independent reviewer: `jobcron-reviewer` for `jobcron`, `chapt-reviewer` for `cha-pt`, and `reviewer-sol` only for the legacy default board. No push, PR, deploy, credential change, or automatic deletion is authorized here.

For diagnostics, apply [diagnostic convergence](diagnostic-convergence.md). Review proportionality and decision utility before implementation minutiae. If an approach is overbuilt or low-value, direct simplify/pivot/abandon rather than serial micro-fixes. The first material correction requires approach reassessment; a second material failure or exhausted design budget requires a strategy pivot or abandonment. Campaign-level terminal retry authorization does not reset a hypothesis's two-generation budget.

External authorization records require the exact owner-approved actor or identity, target and scope, operations and effects, permissions, output custody, and retry/pagination/polling behavior. Add a per-operation request or poll cap, real expiry, or stop condition only when a concrete provider cost, rate, mutation, ambiguity, credential-expiry, or maintenance-window risk justifies it. Do not infer a campaign-wide call or credential-acquisition ceiling, reserved-slot arithmetic, or artificial authorization expiry. A proven local pre-provider/no-submission failure consumes no external-call units and permits at most one retry; each completed provider request, including an error or ambiguous response, is not a free retry. Ambiguous submissions stop for authorized reconciliation. Missing authority or materially broader effects require owner approval. All external-read/write, credential, mutation, deployment, and cutover HUMAN GATEs remain unchanged; diagnostic design/review approval is not execution authorization.

Only an explicit user authorization recorded on the exact card or root campaign may set `terminal_retry_policy: astra-until-approve-v1`; it also requires `ladder_version: glm-review-v2` and `authorization_mode: autonomous`. It is not inherited from a repository, tenant, assignee, model, or another card, and in-flight cards are never silently migrated. The authorization permits only fenced Astra semantic retry: it does not permit external writes or weaken retained human gates for user stop, product decisions, credentials/MFA/prohibited actions, unsafe state, capability limits, or policy/legal/security decisions.

Project-fleet bounded convergence is separately opt-in. The exact card body must carry the two exact full-line authorization declarations; append-only card comments carry a complete ordered `repair_history` JSON snapshot for every transition. Derive counts from those immutable successor snapshots rather than resettable scalar fields: approach position is the approach count, attempt position is the within-approach count, and `infrastructure_retries` is local to that attempt. Repair authority remains on the same card and never transfers to a replacement card. A successor may update only the final pending attempt or append one bounded attempt/approach; prior content cannot disappear or change. Every completed semantic attempt requires a unique candidate commit and independent review run. A new approach records the normalized written strategy, its verified digest, and a distinct material-difference review run; changing a worker, model, prompt, commit, branch, worktree, card, label, or approach ID is insufficient. The v1 ceiling cannot be bypassed by reauthorization: further work needs a newly authorized policy version or explicitly amended limits while preserving prior comments. Human gates and external-write restrictions remain unchanged.
